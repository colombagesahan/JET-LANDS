Frequently Asked Questions

Are the deeds completely clear?

Yes, absolutely. Our Managing Director is an Attorney at Law, and every deed goes through a rigorous 30-year title check.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer interest-free payment plans for a certain period, and we arrange bank loans for long-term payments.

Does the land have water and electricity?

Yes, all our projects come with pipe-borne water, 3-phase electricity, and wide paved roads.
